# V17 expressive presenter study

This pass uses a fixed procedural 3D character to approach the supplied reference's expressiveness. It is a movement study, not a claim of matching the reference's finished visual quality.

Implemented: open LED irises, pupils and catchlights; independent brows; friendly, attentive, surprised and blink morphs; projected gaze targets; softer cloth materials; an authored 7.47-second performance coordinating the head, torso, shoulders, elbows and presenting wrist. Neither approved hand construction block was altered.

Validation: Blender generation and mobile Chromium emulation passed, including exported eye morphs, touch camera controls, imported speech timing and video recording. Joint samples were checked for finite bounded rotations and continuity across the entire take. Front and three-quarter screenshots confirmed the facial meshes remain on the visor.

The demonstration uses newly synthesized diagnostic speech. The user's reference audio was not published. Speech timing remains estimated. The study does not yet provide accurate phoneme alignment, cloth simulation, scene/background matching, a general motion-transfer system or a fully automated news producer.

Next visual priorities: fit the collar/lapels in side view; author rounded lid/smile shapes rather than simple eye compression; improve cinematic lighting and set dressing; refine camera composition and gesture silhouettes.

Final review: build and render workflow 37988017899 succeeded. The output is a 7.5-second 540×960 MP4 with audio at 24 fps. Frames sampled at 4 fps were inspected across the take, together with front, three-quarter and side mobile views. The second face pass reduced visible LED stand-off from the visor. The lowered hand remains near the right frame edge; a wider safety margin and stronger, more varied acting are priorities for the next take.
